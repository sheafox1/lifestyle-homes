How It Works

How building with us works

From the first call to the keys.

1. Discover

We get to know you, your lifestyle and what you want from your home, starting with an initial consultation.

2. Design

We collaborate on design with reviews and refinements, and talk through lot options and recommendations.

3. Plan

Before a shovel hits the ground, we lock in budget, schedule, permits and selections so there are no surprises later.

4. Build and enjoy

We build to an on-time, on-budget commitment, walk the finished home with you and stay close through the warranty period.
